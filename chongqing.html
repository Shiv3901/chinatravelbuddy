🍑 peach / chongqing day trip
🍑 search result

what is it

Massive mountain city built on cliffs above two rivers. Insane skyline that looks like Blade Runner. THE place for authentic Sichuan hotpot. Also: confusing streets that go up, down, and through buildings.

Population: 30+ million. It's huge. You won't see it all. This guide is for one solid day hitting the highlights.

day itinerary (10am - 10pm)

Morning (10am - 1pm)

Hongya Cave 洪崖洞

  • 11-story stilt house structure built into the cliff
  • Looks way cooler from across the river than inside
  • Inside is mostly tourist trap shops - skip it
  • Metro Line 2 to Linjiangmen (临江门), walk 5 min

Then: Walk along Jialing River, cross Qiansi Gate Bridge (千厮门大桥). Get photos of Hongya Cave from the other side. This is the money shot.

Lunch (1pm - 2:30pm)

Hotpot time - this is the main event

Our pick: 朱氏胖子烂火锅 (Zhu's Fat Man Hotpot)

  • Area: 较场口 (Jiaochangkou)
  • Order: 鸳鸯锅 (yuanyang guo) - half spicy, half mild broth
  • Even the "mild" side will kick your ass
  • Cost: ¥80-100 per person
  • Bring stomach medicine. Not joking.

Hotpot ordering tips: Get beef slices, lamb, tofu skin, lotus root, potato, mushrooms. Cook thin meats for 10-15 seconds only. Dipping sauce: sesame oil + garlic + cilantro.

Afternoon (3pm - 6pm)

Metro Line 2 scenic ride

  • The above-ground section between Linjiangmen and Liziba (李子坝)
  • Train literally goes through an apartment building at Liziba station
  • There's a viewing platform below if you want to watch trains go through

Then: Eling Park 鹅岭公园

  • Free entry
  • Best sunset views of the city
  • Less touristy than Nanshan
  • Get there by 5pm for golden hour
Dinner (7pm - 9pm)

Street food at Jiefangbei 解放碑

  • Main pedestrian shopping area in city center
  • The liberation monument itself is whatever
  • Good for: wandering, street snacks, people watching

Try:

  • 酸辣粉 (suan la fen) - spicy sour noodles, ¥10-15
  • 抄手 (chao shou) - Sichuan wontons in chili oil
  • Skip the overpriced restaurants around the square
Night (9pm - 11pm)

Night views from Nanshan 南山

  • Take Didi, ¥40-50 from city center (30 min)
  • One Tree Viewing Platform 一棵树观景台
  • Entry: ¥30
  • See the entire city lit up across the river
  • Best night skyline view in China, arguably

getting around

Metro: ¥2-7 per ride

  • Buy a transit card at any station (deposit ¥20)
  • Or use Alipay to scan in/out
  • Lines 1, 2, and 3 cover most tourist spots

Didi: Works everywhere

  • Set up the app before you arrive
  • ¥15-30 for most trips within the city
  • Worth it when you're tired and confused

Walking: Good luck

  • City is built on mountains. Flat streets don't exist.
  • What looks "close" on the map might be 200 stairs away
  • You will get lost. Accept it. That's part of the experience.

costs (day trip)

Metro card + rides: ¥70

Snacks/water: ¥30

Eling Park: free

Nanshan viewing platform: ¥30

Didi to Nanshan: ¥50

TOTAL: ~¥310 (~$45 USD)

real talk

Good:

  • Hotpot is unreal. Best we had in China.
  • City is visually insane
  • Not as touristy as Beijing/Shanghai
  • Locals are super friendly
  • Cheap compared to east coast cities

Bad:

  • Spicy food WILL destroy you
  • Summer is fucking hot (35°C+)
  • Streets make zero sense
  • Everything is stairs
  • Air quality can be rough

Worth it? 100%. Chongqing has more personality than most Chinese cities. Even if you're only passing through, stop for a day and eat hotpot.

survival tips

  • Bring toilet paper. Public bathrooms don't have it.
  • Learn to say 不要辣 (bù yào là) - "not spicy". Though they might ignore you.
  • Your stomach will hurt. Accept it. Bring medicine.
  • Get Alipay or WeChat Pay working, or bring cash. Cards don't work most places.
  • Download offline map. Google doesn't work, Baidu or Amap does.
  • Umbrella - weather changes fast.

getting to/from jiuzhaigou

If you're doing both places:

  • Bus: ~10 hours, ¥200-250. Long but scenic mountain roads.
  • Flight: No direct flights. You'd fly through Chengdu.
  • Better route: Chengdu → Jiuzhaigou → Chengdu → Chongqing (high-speed train, 2 hours)

We did Chongqing after Jiuzhaigou as a stopover before flying out. Worked well.
