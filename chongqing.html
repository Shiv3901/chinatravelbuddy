Chongqing Day Trip

Mountain megacity known for hotpot and dramatic skylines. Full day itinerary.

Search Result

Overview

Chongqing is a municipality of over 30 million people built into the mountains where the Yangtze and Jialing rivers meet. The city is known for its dramatic verticality - streets that go up, down, and through buildings - as well as being the origin point of authentic Sichuan hotpot.

The city has a distinctive aesthetic that's often described as cyberpunk, particularly at night when the layered skyline lights up across the river valleys.

Day Itinerary

10:00 - 13:00 / Morning

Hongya Cave (洪崖洞)

  • 11-story traditional stilt house structure built into the cliff face
  • The exterior view from across the river is significantly better than the interior (which is mostly tourist shops)
  • Take Metro Line 2 to Linjiangmen station, 5-minute walk

Then: Qiansi Gate Bridge

  • Walk along the Jialing River to Qiansi Gate Bridge (千厮门大桥)
  • Cross the bridge for the best photographs of Hongya Cave
  • This is the iconic view you've seen in photos
13:00 - 14:30 / Lunch

Hotpot

This is the main culinary experience. Recommended restaurant:

Zhu's Fat Man Hotpot (朱氏胖子烂火锅) in the Jiaochangkou (较场口) area.

  • Order the 鸳鸯锅 (yuanyang guo) - a divided pot with one spicy and one mild broth
  • Note: Even the "mild" side is spicy by most standards
  • Cost: ¥80-100 per person
  • Recommended items: beef slices, lamb, tofu skin, lotus root, potato, mushrooms
  • Dipping sauce: sesame oil with minced garlic

Important: Bring stomach medication. Chongqing hotpot is significantly spicier than other regional variants. This is not an exaggeration.

15:00 - 18:00 / Afternoon

Metro Line 2 Scenic Section

  • The above-ground section of Metro Line 2 passes through a residential building at Liziba station (李子坝)
  • There's a viewing platform below the station to watch trains pass through
  • Worth a brief stop for the novelty

Eling Park (鹅岭公园)

  • Free entry
  • Best sunset views of the city
  • Less crowded than the more famous Nanshan viewpoint
  • Arrive by 17:00 for golden hour
19:00 - 21:00 / Evening

Jiefangbei (解放碑)

  • Main pedestrian shopping district in the city center
  • Good for street food and people watching
  • Try: 酸辣粉 (suan la fen) - spicy and sour noodles, approximately ¥10-15
  • Try: 抄手 (chao shou) - Sichuan-style wontons in chili oil
21:00 - 23:00 / Night

Nanshan Night View

  • Take Didi (ride-hailing app), approximately ¥40-50 from city center, 30 minutes
  • One Tree Viewing Platform (一棵树观景台)
  • Entry fee: ¥30
  • Panoramic view of the entire city illuminated across the river
  • Arguably the best urban night view in China

Getting Around

Metro: ¥2-7 per ride. Purchase a transit card at any station (¥20 deposit). The system is extensive and covers most tourist areas.

Didi: Ride-hailing works well throughout the city. ¥15-30 for most trips. Recommended for Nanshan and when tired from walking.

Walking: Be prepared for stairs. The city is built on mountains, and what appears close on a map may require climbing significant elevation. This is part of the experience but can be exhausting.

Cost Breakdown

Metro card and rides: ¥70

Snacks and water: ¥30

Eling Park: Free

Total: approximately ¥310 (~$45 USD)

Assessment

Strengths

  • Hotpot is exceptional - arguably the best in China
  • Visually dramatic cityscape
  • Less touristed than Beijing or Shanghai
  • Locals are notably friendly
  • Affordable compared to east coast cities

Challenges

  • Spicy food will affect your digestion
  • Summer temperatures exceed 35°C with high humidity
  • Street layout is confusing
  • Significant elevation changes between areas
  • Air quality can be poor

Verdict: Worth visiting. Chongqing has more character than many Chinese cities. Even as a stopover, dedicate at least one day for hotpot and the night skyline.

Practical Tips

  • Bring toilet paper. Public restrooms typically don't provide it.
  • Learn the phrase 不要辣 (bù yào là) meaning "not spicy." Staff may or may not comply.
  • Set up Alipay or WeChat Pay before arrival. Card acceptance is limited.
  • Download Baidu Maps or Amap for navigation. Google Maps doesn't function in China.
  • Summer visits require heat preparation - temperatures regularly exceed 35°C.

Connecting to Jiuzhaigou

If visiting both destinations:

  • Direct bus: Approximately 10 hours, ¥200-250. Long but scenic route through mountains.
  • Recommended route: Travel via Chengdu. High-speed train between Chengdu and Chongqing takes only 2 hours. Then take the bus from Chengdu to Jiuzhaigou.
