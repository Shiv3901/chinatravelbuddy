🍑 peach / jiuzhaigou national park
🍑 search result

what is it

UNESCO World Heritage site in Sichuan province. Insane turquoise lakes, massive waterfalls, Tibetan villages scattered through the valleys. Think: Avatar mountains but real and accessible.

Best time: Sept-Oct (fall colors are unreal) or June-Aug (summer, greener)

Avoid: Chinese national holidays. Seriously. The crowds will ruin it. Golden Week (Oct 1-7) is the worst.

getting there

You're probably coming from Chengdu. Options:

  • Bus: 8-10 hours, ¥150 one-way. Leaves from Chadianzi Bus Station (茶店子客运站). Bumpy mountain roads. Bring motion sickness pills.
  • Flight: 50 min to Jiuzhai Huanglong Airport (JZH), then 2-hour bus to the park. More expensive but way less painful.
  • Private car: ¥400-600 one-way, 7-8 hours. Can arrange through hostels. Worth it if you're splitting with others.

We took the bus. Bumpy as hell but saved money. The mountain scenery on the way is actually pretty sick.

the itinerary (3 days)

Day 1: Arrive + Rize Valley
  • Check into hostel in Zhangzha Town (the town right outside the park)
  • Afternoon: Enter park, take the bus up to Rize Valley (日则沟)
  • See: Arrow Bamboo Lake, Panda Lake, Five Flower Lake (五花海 - this one is insane)
  • Walk between some stops instead of waiting for bus - better views, fewer people
  • Evening: Tibetan dinner in town. ¥40-60 per person. Get the yak meat.
Day 2: Zechawa Valley + Shuzheng Valley
  • Early start - be at the gate by 7am
  • Morning: Zechawa Valley (则查洼沟) - Long Lake (longest in the park) + Five Colored Pool
  • Afternoon: Shuzheng Valley (树正沟) - Nuorilang Falls (诺日朗瀑布), Reed Lake
  • Pro tip: Walk the wooden boardwalks between Shuzheng stops. Buses are crowded af.
  • Full day in park - bring snacks and water
Day 3: Repeat favorites + leave
  • Morning: Go back to your favorite spots (we went back to Five Flower Lake at sunrise - worth it)
  • Afternoon: Bus back to Chengdu or head to your next destination

where to stay

Everything is in Zhangzha Town (漳扎镇), right outside the park entrance.

Budget (¥60-100/night):

  • Jiuzhai Self-Tour Youth Hostel - clean, hot water, met cool people
  • Most guesthouses in the main strip are fine. Just check for hot water.

Mid-range (¥200-400):

  • Various 3-star hotels in town
  • Sheraton if you want western comfort (but why tho)

We stayed at the hostel. Clean enough, hot water worked, owner helped us book the bus back. No complaints.

costs breakdown

Total for 3 days (per person):

Park entrance: ¥169 (April-Nov) / ¥80 (Dec-Mar)

Park shuttle bus: ¥90

Accommodation: ¥180 (3 nights at hostel)

Food: ¥200 (eating cheap)

Bus Chengdu↔Jiuzhaigou: ¥300 round trip

Random stuff: ¥100

TOTAL: ~¥1,000 (~$140 USD)

You could do it cheaper if you really tried. Or way more if you fly and stay at nice hotels.

what to bring

  • Layers - morning is cold (10°C), afternoon gets warm (20°C+)
  • Sunscreen - altitude means you burn fast
  • Snacks + water - food in park is expensive and meh
  • Cash - some places don't take mobile payment
  • Motion sickness pills - if taking the bus
  • Comfortable shoes - you'll walk 15-20km a day
  • Portable charger - you'll take a million photos

things we wish we knew

  • Download offline maps before you go. Signal in the park is garbage.
  • Don't buy water inside the park (¥10 vs ¥2 outside). Stock up in town.
  • Wooden walkways get slippery when wet. Saw someone eat shit.
  • Altitude is 2,000-3,000m. Take it easy on day 1 if you're not used to it.
  • Best photos are early morning before the tour groups arrive.
  • The park is HUGE. Don't try to see everything in one day.
  • Nuorilang Falls is underwhelming if you've seen big waterfalls elsewhere. Lakes are the main event.

is it worth it?

Yeah. One of the most beautiful places we've been in China. The lakes really are that blue - it's not photoshop. Just don't go during national holidays and you'll have a great time.

If you only have 2 days, you can still do it - just pick one valley per day and skip the third day.
