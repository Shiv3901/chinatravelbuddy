Jiuzhaigou National Park

UNESCO World Heritage site in Sichuan Province. 3-day itinerary.

Search Result

Overview

Jiuzhaigou is a nature reserve and national park in the Sichuan province of China. Known for its colorful lakes, multi-level waterfalls, snow-capped peaks, and Tibetan villages, it's one of the most visually striking destinations in China.

Best time to visit: September to October for fall colors, or June to August for summer greenery.

Avoid: Chinese national holidays, especially Golden Week (October 1-7). Crowds of 30,000+ visitors per day will significantly impact your experience.

Getting There

Most visitors arrive from Chengdu. Three main options:

  • Bus: 8-10 hours, approximately ¥150 one-way. Departs from Chadianzi Bus Station (茶店子客运站). Scenic mountain roads but can be uncomfortable. Bring motion sickness medication.
  • Flight: 50 minutes to Jiuzhai Huanglong Airport (JZH), then a 2-hour bus transfer to the park entrance. More expensive but significantly more comfortable.
  • Private car: ¥400-600 one-way, 7-8 hours. Can be arranged through hostels or travel agencies. Worth considering if traveling with others.

3-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival and Rize Valley
  • Check into accommodation in Zhangzha Town (the town outside the park entrance)
  • Afternoon: Enter park and take the shuttle bus to Rize Valley (日则沟)
  • Key stops: Arrow Bamboo Lake, Panda Lake, Five Flower Lake (五花海) - the most photographed lake in the park
  • Walk between some bus stops instead of waiting - better views and fewer crowds
  • Evening: Tibetan dinner in town, ¥40-60 per person
Day 2: Zechawa Valley and Shuzheng Valley
  • Start early - be at the park entrance by 7am
  • Morning: Zechawa Valley (则查洼沟) - Long Lake (the largest in the park) and Five Colored Pool
  • Afternoon: Shuzheng Valley (树正沟) - Nuorilang Falls (诺日朗瀑布), Reed Lake, Shuzheng Villages
  • The wooden boardwalks between Shuzheng stops are worth walking - buses are often crowded
  • Full day in park - bring snacks and water
Day 3: Revisit and Departure
  • Morning: Return to your favorite spots (recommend Five Flower Lake at sunrise)
  • Afternoon: Depart for Chengdu or your next destination

Where to Stay

All accommodation is in Zhangzha Town (漳扎镇), located just outside the park entrance.

Budget (¥60-100/night): Several hostels available. Jiuzhai Self-Tour Youth Hostel is reliable - clean rooms, hot water, helpful staff who can assist with bus bookings.

Mid-range (¥200-400): Various 3-star hotels in the main strip. Quality varies.

Upscale: Sheraton and other international brands available if comfort is a priority.

Cost Breakdown

Park entrance fee: ¥169 (April-November) / ¥80 (December-March)

Park shuttle bus: ¥90

Accommodation: ¥180 (3 nights at budget hostel)

Food: ¥200 (eating at local restaurants)

Bus Chengdu to Jiuzhaigou return: ¥300

Miscellaneous: ¥100

Total: approximately ¥1,000 (~$140 USD)

Essential Tips

  • Download offline maps before arriving. Mobile signal inside the park is unreliable.
  • Purchase water and snacks in Zhangzha Town. Prices inside the park are 3-5x higher.
  • Wooden boardwalks become slippery when wet. Appropriate footwear is important.
  • Altitude ranges from 2,000-3,000 meters. Take it easy on the first day if you're not acclimatized.
  • Best photography conditions are early morning before tour groups arrive.
  • The park is extensive. Don't attempt to see everything in one day.

Verdict

Jiuzhaigou is one of the most beautiful natural destinations in China. The lakes genuinely are as blue and clear as photographs suggest. Avoid national holidays, arrive early, and you'll have an exceptional experience. Can be done in 2 days if time is limited - allocate one valley per day.
